# Privacy Policy for DocuBox

**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 6, 2026

DocuBox ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a 100% offline, privacy-first productivity suite. We believe your documents, files, and personal data belong solely to you.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
- **No Personal Data Collected:** DocuBox does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, device identifiers, or user activity.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use any feature of DocuBox.
- **No Analytics or Trackers:** DocuBox contains **zero** third-party analytics libraries (no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Facebook SDK) and **zero** advertisement SDKs.

---

### 2. 100% On-Device Processing
All features within DocuBox operate completely offline on your device's local hardware:
- **Intelligent Scanner:** Camera scanning, perspective correction, and image filtering are performed on-device.
- **Offline OCR:** Text recognition is powered entirely by on-device Google ML Kit models without connecting to external servers.
- **Document & Spreadsheet Reader:** DOCX, XLSX, CSV, and PDF rendering processes files strictly inside local memory.
- **Universal Archiver:** Compression and extraction (ZIP, 7Z, RAR, TAR, GZ) take place exclusively on your device storage.
- **Military Vault:** AES-256 encryption and decryption are executed locally with your personal passphrase. We have no master key or server backdoor.
- **QR & Barcode Tools:** Scanning and QR generation operate locally without internet transmission.

---

### 3. Device Permissions and Usage
DocuBox only requests permissions strictly necessary to perform functions requested directly by you:
- **Camera (`CAMERA`):** Required only when you use the document scanner or QR code scanner. Camera frames are processed in real time and are never transmitted externally.
- **Storage / Media Access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** Required to allow you to select, view, compress, extract, and save documents and archives across your device.
- **No Internet Required:** Core features function seamlessly without an active internet connection.

---

### 4. Third-Party Sharing
Because we collect no data, we do not share, sell, or disclose any user data to third parties, advertising networks, or government entities.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
DocuBox does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 6. Updates to This Policy
If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted in this repository with a revised "Last Updated" date.
